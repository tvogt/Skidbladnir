# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version: craftable folded longship (Forge, longship materials + 2 Serpent trophies), unfold with the game's longship placement rules, fold with the hammer's remove action (empty hold, nobody aboard; level and health are kept).
- Level 2 (upgrade with the Moder trophy): permanent fair wind while someone is aboard.
- Recipe, station, weight and fair-wind level configurable and synced from the server.
